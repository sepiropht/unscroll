Privacy policy

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Unscroll is a local-first, open-source screen-time app. The developer does not collect your usage, does not show ads, and does not use trackers. This page describes what stays on your devices, what optional sync does, and how Unscroll Cloud payments work.

On your devices

Measurements, limits, websites, reminders, and settings are stored on the device where you use Unscroll. There is no account to create to use the apps. You can export or import a JSON backup yourself. Uninstalling the app removes what was stored on that device.

What the apps can see

Usage access, when you grant it, is used to count time spent in each app and to know which app is in front, so a limit can show the pause screen. Display over other apps is used only to show that pause screen. Website tracking is optional and off by default: when you turn it on, after an in-app explanation and your consent, an accessibility service reads only the address bar of known browsers to estimate time per site. It does not read page content, messages, passwords, or what you type, and it does not control the screen. Pairing uses a shown QR code or a pasted link; the app does not use the camera. Nothing in this section is sent off the device unless you turn on optional sync.

Optional end-to-end encrypted sync

Sync is off until you turn it on. You choose the server: your own, or Unscroll Cloud. The device encrypts the backup with a key that stays on your devices (AES-GCM) and sends only ciphertext. The server stores an anonymous account identifier (a hash of your token) and that ciphertext. It cannot read your measurements, limits, or settings.

If you stop using a hosted account (the trial ends without a subscription, or you cancel), the encrypted server copy is kept for 30 days and then deleted. Data on your devices is not deleted by that process. You can keep a JSON export or point the apps at a server you host.

Unscroll Cloud and Stripe

Unscroll Cloud is the hosted sync server. It is optional. Self-hosted sync is free. Cloud costs €2.99/month or €24/year, with a 14-day trial and no card required to start. Payments are processed by Stripe. We send Stripe only an anonymous account id. Stripe also collects whatever you enter in Stripe Checkout (for example a payment method, name, email, and billing address). Usage data never goes to Stripe. End-to-end encryption is unchanged if you subscribe.

This website

This site does not set advertising cookies and does not embed an audience tracker. A language preference may be saved in your browser (localStorage) so the page can stay in English or French. Server logs on the host may record a standard request (date, IP address, page) for operation and abuse prevention, and are not used for advertising.
